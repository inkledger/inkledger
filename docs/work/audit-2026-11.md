# Status: security and consolidation audit (November 2026)

## Goal and completion criteria
Full audit (security, bugs, efficiency, enterprise use) of inkledger, src-tauri, scripts/CI and inkledger-modules. Fixes only, no new features, zero regressions. Done when every confirmed register item is fixed with a test that failed before, or dismissed with a reason, and the full gate is green.

## Constraints and out of scope
- No push, deploy, signing or real service. Test servers always with PROVIDERS_READ_ONLY=1 JIRA_READ_ONLY=1.
- Never touch prod (jtt stack). Migrations only new and additive. Mods at 1.0.0, never `build` in inkledger-modules.
- Public repo: the register of open findings stays in `studio/` (gitignored); here only batches, verification and fixed items.
- Out of scope: SSO, audit log, granular RBAC, configurable quotas, mod sandbox.
